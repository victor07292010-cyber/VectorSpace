A little friendly competition.
Even offline.

Save the collection once and bring 27 offline games with you, including 22 with computer opponents. No account, app installation, or internet needed to play locally.

  1. Download the collection

    Use Download offline on the home page. Save the VectorSpace ZIP somewhere you can find it.

  2. Extract the whole ZIP

    On Windows, right-click and choose Extract All. On a Mac, double-click the ZIP. Keep all the extracted files together.

  3. Open & play

    Open the VectorSpace folder and double-click index.html. Your browser opens the collection. Choose a solo or computer game.

Already looking at the downloaded folder? You’re ready. Open the game library. Avoid opening the HTML inside a ZIP preview: extract it first so every game can load.

Your computer is ready to play.

Try Color Clash, Crazy Eights, Go Fish, Sea Battle, Connect Four, Pig Dice, Tic-Tac-Toe, Face Finder, Reversi, Dots & Boxes, Liar’s Dice, or Rock Paper Scissors. Choose Vs computer where a player mode is offered.

Prefer your own pace? Solitaire, Shut the Box, and Higher or Lower are ready for a solo session. Computer opponents run on your device; no AI subscription or cloud connection is needed.

Room codes need a connection.

Same-screen games work offline where offered. Online rooms need internet and friends on their own devices. The host must keep the room open.

Imposter, Mafia, and Sketch Party are social games for real groups. Use VectorSpace online for the easiest room experience.
